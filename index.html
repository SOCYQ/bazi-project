Your Day Master (the day stem) represents your core self. The four pillars together show the elemental energies present at your birth — their balance shapes personality, strengths, and the types of energy you naturally attract or need.

Luck Pillars (大運 Dà Yùn) are 10-year energy cycles that overlay your birth chart. Each pillar brings a different elemental energy into your life. Your current pillar is highlighted in blue.

The two layers explained

Every day has two independent ratings. The Day Officer is universal — identical for every person on earth. Your personal score is unique to your Bazi chart. The coloured verdict synthesises both into one recommendation.

The six verdicts

Strong dayUniversal officer is favourable AND personal score is high. Act boldly.
Good dayOne layer is positive with no major negatives. Decent day to move things forward.
Proceed with careMixed signals — one layer positive, the other cautious. Low-stakes activities only.
Avoid todayBoth layers are negative. Rest and routine tasks only.
ConflictingOfficer and personal score disagree. Personal matters only — avoid public commitments.
Rest dayNeutral across both layers. Fine for routine and admin.

How the personal score is calculated

Your 8 birth characters are compared against that day's Stem + Branch pair. Each interaction is scored:

Stem harmony (天干合)+2 points
Branch harmony (地支合)+2 points
Favourable element match+1 point
Stem clash−2 points
Branch clash (地支冲)−3 points

Typical scores range from about −6 to +7. Scores of +3 or above are auspicious; −2 or below are inauspicious.
